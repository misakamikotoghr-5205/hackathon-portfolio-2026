# Lumen Shift

**Short description:** Shift the shutters. Rotate the signal. Find one clear pattern.

Lumen Shift is a quiet browser logic puzzle built for Game Off 2026's SHIFT theme. Circularly shift each shutter's bit pattern, watch the incoming signal rotate and XOR through the instrument, and match both the marked checkpoints and final receiver.

Eight handcrafted boards introduce wrapping, rotating signals, intermediate readings and fixed shutters. There is no timer. Undo/redo, exact-search hints, optional locally synthesized tones, browser saves and replay-validated import/export let you experiment at your own pace. The hint searches your current orientation space; it is not a scripted answer or a language-model call.

## Controls

Use arrow keys to select/shift shutters, or the labeled on-screen buttons on touch. Two lit bits cancel through XOR. A row's blue arrow rotates its incoming signal before the amber shutter acts. Hints are labeled assistance; export a replay if you want to keep an attempt before resetting.
